## Title A/B test

The owner is A/B testing titles: {{count}} titles, one thumbnail per title, shown to different viewers of the same video.
The titles, in order:

{{titles}}

- Concept N is the thumbnail for title N. Put the title in the concept as `"title": "<the title, exactly as listed>"`; a concept
  whose `title` is missing, changed or out of order makes the whole plan unusable.
- **The thumbnail never repeats its title.** The viewer reads the title right beside the thumbnail, so the words on the image
  (`text`) must say what the title does not: the stakes, the twist, a verdict, a number the title leaves out. A `text` made of
  the title's own words, or a paraphrase of them, makes the plan unusable. No text at all is fine when the picture carries it.
- Each concept should pick up what its title promises (a title about a result wants the result; a title that asks a question
  wants the tension) while the picture stays honest to the video. Different titles usually mean different concepts, but they
  are still the same video: do not invent a moment, a number or a face expression the video does not have.
- The rules above still hold: every concept uses a different archetype, and different frames and faces where there are several.
