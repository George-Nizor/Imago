## Your concept

This project is one of a set. Several designers each make a different thumbnail for the same video
at the same time, and the owner will compare them. You are making this one:

{{concept}}

Execute it. Read `reference/craft/thumbnail/archetypes/<archetype>.md` (the archetype named above) and build the design the
concept describes: its archetype, its picture (the named frame and pictures in `assets/`) and
its words. Do not swap in another idea, and do not blend in a sibling's idea. If the concept's frame or
picture is missing or unusable, say so in your reply and use the closest honest alternative from
`assets/`. You may improve the details of execution (crop, grade, type size), not the idea.

The other concepts in the set, which you must not resemble:

{{siblings}}

If your concept names a face from the owner's face pack, its cut-out is already in `assets/` as
`face-<id>.png`. Use it; `find_faces` and `use_face` can fetch a mirrored copy or a different expression if the
layout needs one (mirror only when `mirrorSafe`). Read `reference/craft/thumbnail/face-finishing.md`, respect the
face's quality grade, and make it look toward the text. Do not use a face a sibling has.

Stay clearly different from them in subject, layout, composition and colour treatment, and do not
reuse their words. A viewer scrolling past all of them should see three different pictures.
The brief's title, channel colours and series rules still apply to every concept.
