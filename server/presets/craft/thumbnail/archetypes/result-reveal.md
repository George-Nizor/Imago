# result-reveal

The end result, shown big and clean, with one word that frames it. The proof is the picture.

## Fits when
The video builds, makes, wins, fixes, grows or finishes something and the result can be seen in a
frame or picture the video itself shows: a finished build, a score screen, a graph that went up, a
product, a dish, a room.

## Layout
- 16:9: the result fills 60-80% of the canvas, centred or off-centre on a simple dark or coloured
  ground, with a keyline or a soft shadow. The maker's face, if any, is a smaller secondary figure
  (25-35% of the height) in a lower corner looking at the result. Text: one word or a number on the
  empty side.
- 9:16: the result large in the upper centre (inside the 3:4 crop, away from the right rail), text
  underneath it. A face is optional and small.
- 1:1: the result alone, centred, nearly filling the square, 0-2 words.

## Text
A verdict or a stake: "FINISHED", "IT WORKS", "WINNER", "AT LAST". A number only if the kit gives it
(`number` archetype fits when the number is the point). Do not label what is visible
("THE RESULT").

## Fails when
The result is not what the video shows (a stand-in or stock); it is too small to read at 200 px
wide; it has fine detail that turns to noise (simplify, crop, enlarge the best part); the face
competes with it.
