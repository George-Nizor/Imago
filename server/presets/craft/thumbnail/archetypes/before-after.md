# before-after

Two states of the same thing, split, so the change is understood in under a second.

## Fits when
The video transforms something: a room, a car, a skill, a score, a design, a layout, a body of
work, a bug. You have two real pictures of it, before and after, from the video or the owner's files.
Both sides must be real. Never fabricate the "before" or the "after".

## Layout
- 16:9: a clean vertical split at 50% (or a 45/55 split with the "after" larger), a diagonal
  `clip-path` or a thin bright divider 6-10 px wide in the accent. Same framing and scale on
  both sides so the change is the only difference. Grade the before cooler and duller, the after
  warmer and brighter. One label each side, one word, at the top of each half, or one word on the
  divider.
- 9:16: stack top (before) and bottom (after) inside the 3:4 centre crop, with the divider at mid
  height. Labels short ("BEFORE" / "AFTER" is acceptable; better "DAY 1" / "DAY 30" only if the video
  says so).
- 1:1: split down the middle or on the diagonal, one label each.

## Text
Labels, not sentences. Optionally one verdict word across the divider.

## Fails when
The two sides are different crops or lighting so the difference is unclear; the change is too
small to see at feed size (zoom into it); there is a face as well (drop it or keep it for one side
only); the "before" is made up.
