# versus

Two things or two people in a face-off. The viewer picks a side.

## Fits when
The video compares, tests or pits two things against each other: two products, tools, methods,
people, places. Both have a clear picture from the video or the owner's files.

## Layout
- 16:9: two subjects facing each other on either side, equal in size, with a strong divider in the
  middle: a diagonal slash or a "VS" mark 120-200 px, in the accent. Give each side its own
  colour (two accent colours, or accent and white) behind it. A single short word under or between,
  if any. Keep the badge corner clear.
- 9:16: the two stacked top and bottom, or diagonally, inside the 3:4 centre crop, with the VS
  mark at the middle.
- 1:1: left and right, or top and bottom, equal halves.

## Text
"VS" is a graphic, not a message; add at most one word of stake ("WHO WINS?", "ONE LEFT") only if the
title does not already ask it. Do not name both subjects if the title does.

## Face
If people, both look toward the centre; if one is the owner, they are larger or sharper than the other.

## Fails when
One side is much brighter or bigger by accident; the subjects are unrecognisable at feed size; a
side shows something that never appears in the video; there is a third element.
