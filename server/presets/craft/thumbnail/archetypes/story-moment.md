# story-moment

A single frame from the video that tells a story by itself, graded and cropped like a film still.

## Fits when
The video is narrative, a vlog, a documentary, a journey, an event, a build log. There is a frame
with action, scale or atmosphere (a person in the middle of something, a view, a mess, a crowd) rather
than a posed face. Also the safe choice when no frame has a strong expression.

## Layout
- 16:9: the frame full-bleed, cropped to the action, graded (contrast 1.1-1.2, saturation 1.1-1.2,
  a subtle warm/cool split with gradient overlays) with a vignette and a dark gradient at the bottom
  or the side where text goes. Text 1-3 words, large, anchored on that gradient, away from the badge.
- 9:16: crop the best vertical slice of the frame (move the focus with `object-position`), keep the
  subject inside the 3:4 centre crop and clear of the rails; text low-middle on a gradient.
- 1:1: a square crop of the action, one or two words.

## Text
A line from the story at its most intense, or the stake: "THE LAST DAY", "IT BROKE", "ALONE". Use the
speaker's own words from the hooks when you quote.

## Fails when
The frame is dark or flat and the grade cannot save it (choose another); the action is too
small in the frame (crop in); the text sits over the busiest part; a stock photo stands in for the
event.
