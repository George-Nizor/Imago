# reaction

A big expressive face, a short word or two, the thing it reacts to small in the other half.

## Fits when
The video is a first look, a test, a surprise, a fail, a verdict, a challenge: something happens
to a person. The owner has (or the kit has) a frame with a clear expression: surprise, laugh,
intense, disbelief.

## Face source
A frame of the video that shows the reaction wins. With none, the owner's face pack: `find_faces` for the
expression the idea needs (surprise, shock, laugh, intense) at `minGrade: "hd"` or better, because the face is
big here. Choose the gaze that points at the text or the thing reacted to; mirror when `mirrorSafe`.

## Layout
- 16:9: face 55-75% of the height on one side, bleeding off the bottom and that side. The object or
  scene it reacts to fills the other side at 30-45% of the width, sharp, with a keyline. Text over
  the empty third, 1-3 words, two lines at most.
- 9:16: face in the upper-middle (eyes at 30-38% of the height, inside the 3:4 crop), 35-45% of the
  height. Text below it, under the chin, inside the centre crop. The object sits to the side or
  below the text, never in the right rail.
- 1:1: face 60% of the canvas, centred slightly low, object small at a corner that is not a corner
  of the app UI (podcast covers shrink; keep the face as the whole idea). 1-2 words or none.

## Text
A reaction word the face already implies ("NO WAY", "WHAT?", "DONE.") or a verdict ("FAIL", "WORTH IT?"). Do not
caption the face with what it feels in words that repeat the expression ("SHOCKED").

## Face
Eyes toward the object or the text, mouth open for surprise or laugh, brows up. Tight crop. Rim
light in the accent on the side facing the text. See `face-finishing.md`.

## Fails when
The expression is neutral or faked by distortion; the face looks down at a screen; the reacted-to
thing is unreadable at feed size (then enlarge it or drop it); the stock thing contradicts the
video; the text repeats the title.
