# number

One big figure, the proof or the stake, with the subject behind or beside it.

## Fits when
The title or the video turns on a quantity: a price, a count, a time, a percentage, days, a score,
a rank, a multiplier. The number must come from the brief or a kit hook. If there is no sourced
number, do not use this archetype.

## Layout
- 16:9: the number is the biggest element, 300-520 px tall, in a heavy face (Anton, Archivo at 900
  and wdth 62-75, League Gothic, Bebas Neue), with one short unit or word beside it at a quarter of its
  size. The subject (object or face) sits behind or beside, cropped tight, darkened a little so
  the number wins. Number in the accent or white on a dark ground, with a stroke or shadow.
- 9:16: the number fills the width of the centre crop (up to about 520 px wide on the 720 canvas),
  stacked above or below the subject. Use two to four characters at most.
- 1:1: the number nearly fills the canvas with the subject faintly behind.

## Text
The figure plus at most one unit/word ("$0", "30 DAYS", "100x", "#1"). Write it as the video says it.
Do not round, improve, or compare ("2x BETTER") unless the video does.

## Fails when
The number is invented or exaggerated; there are three numbers; the unit is as loud as the figure;
the number's colour is the same as the background's mid-tones; it is so wide it enters the badge zone.
