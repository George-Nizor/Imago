# object-hero

The object, the product, the place or the screen is the star, bold and close, without a person.

## Fits when
The video is about a thing: a gadget, a build, a tool, a game, a screen capture, a place, a
food. There is a clean picture of it from the video, or a cut-out of it.

## Layout
- 16:9: the object at 60-80% of the height, lit and cut out (`cut_out`) or cropped tightly, placed on
  one third line with a bold colour field or gradient behind it and a rim light. A short word on the
  other side. For a screen frame, crop to the one region that matters, enlarge it and add a keyline.
- 9:16: the object large in the upper-middle of the centre crop, text below. Avoid objects that
  run into the right rail.
- 1:1: the object centred, nearly filling it, a short word or none.

## Text
A short name, a verdict or a quality ("FAST", "HANDMADE", "TOO LOUD"). Not the product's full
name if the title has it.

## Fails when
The object is small, or reads as a screenshot with tiny text; the background is a busy
photo that fights it; a stock photo of the object stands in for the real one; a fake logo or label.
