# Podcast thumbnails (1:1)

YouTube shows podcast episodes and playlists with a square thumbnail. The canvas is 1280x1280,
exported at 3840x3840. It appears small (often 60-200 px) in lists, with the title beside it, and
larger in the player. The show's thumbnails sit in a column or grid together, so the series look
matters more than on video.

- **One subject, centred or on a third line.** A face (the host or the guest) cropped to the
  shoulders, or a single object/symbol. Fill 60-75% of the square.
- **1-3 words, or none.** Text is the guest's name, the episode's one idea, or the number of the
  episode. Large: 220-380 px cap height on 1280. Nothing in the four corners: players round and
  overlay them.
- **No small print.** No "PODCAST", host names, logos or taglines at small sizes. A show mark, if
  the owner supplies one, goes at a consistent corner at 12-15% of the width and is allowed to be
  small, but the thumbnail must work without it.
- **Series consistency.** Keep the grounds, type and framing of one show recognisable. When the
  project is in a series with a style reference, the series section decides.
- **Two guests:** split vertically with a clear divider; keep both faces at least 35% of the height.
- Archetypes that suit: `reaction`, `object-hero`, `number`, `curiosity-gap`, `story-moment`. For
  `before-after` and `versus` use a left/right or top/bottom split. Layouts per archetype are in
  the archetype files under "1:1".
- Colours: one saturated ground and one bright accent; podcast apps place the image beside a white
  or dark interface, so the edge should not be white or black.
