# Honesty, and the title-thumbnail pairing

## Honesty: show only what the video delivers

A click that feels misled is followed by an early exit, and watch time weighs more than the click.
So the image makes a promise the video keeps.

- Show only what the video shows or says. If the title is "I built a rocket in my garage", the image
  shows the garage, the rocket or the builder, never a stock NASA launch.
- Prefer the video's own material: the kit's frames and pictures, the owner's uploads. Stock images
  (`search_images`, "Take it further" turns) may set a mood or a texture behind the subject. They
  must never contradict the claim, depict the result, or stand in for a person, place or product
  the video is about.
- No invented numbers, prices, counts, dates, ranks, results or quotes. A number on the image comes
  from the brief or a kit hook, word for word. If you have none, use an archetype that needs none.
- No fake interface: no fabricated play buttons, notification badges, "LIVE", "NEW", verified ticks,
  fake views or timestamps. No arrows or circles pointing at things that are not there.
- No exaggerated expressions faked by distortion (stretched faces, warped eyes). Pick a frame that
  already has the expression; finish it with light and crop (`face-finishing.md`).
- Do not imply a person said something they did not. A quote on the image is a kit hook, verbatim.
- If the brief is too thin to say what the video delivers, make the safest honest image (the
  subject, the topic word) and say in your reply what you would need to be bolder.

## Pairing: the image says what the title does not

The title sits right under the thumbnail in every feed, so the two are read together as one
message. Split the work:

- The title carries the topic and the specifics. The image carries emotion, the stake, the
  proof or the question.
- The image text is 1-4 words (2-4 on a vertical cover). Never restate the title, not in other
  words either: if the title is "I tried every AI image tool", the image text is not "AI IMAGE TOOLS".
  It might be "NOT WORTH IT", "WINNER", or nothing.
- Good text answers one of these: How did it end? How much / how many? What went wrong? What is the
  catch? Who wins? The check `text.repeatsTitle` measures the overlap; treat a warning as a rewrite.
- No text at all is legitimate when the picture is the hook (an object, a face, a result). Do not
  add words to fill space.
- Words are cut short and said loud: a verb, a number, a verdict, a short question. No articles, no
  sentences, no punctuation except `?` or `!` when it is the point. Uppercase unless the face is
  hand-drawn.
- One word can carry the whole image. If a second word does not change the meaning, cut it.

## Order of work

1. Say in one sentence what the video delivers (from the brief, hooks, chapters).
2. Say what the title already tells. The image must add something else.
3. Choose the archetype, then the picture, then the words. Only then write HTML.
