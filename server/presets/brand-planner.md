## Planning with a brand

The owner chose the brand above for this video. It fixes colour and type only; the archetypes, the
pictures and the layouts stay yours to choose. When you write each concept's `idea`, describe it in the
brand's palette (name the colours of its grounds, accents and text by their brand names), so the
concepts differ in idea and composition while all belonging to one identity. Do not plan around a
colour outside the palette. Brand assets are in `assets/brand-*` (and listed above); they are optional
and are not pictures from the video, so never use one as a concept's `frame` or `pictures`.
